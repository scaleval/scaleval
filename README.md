**An open discipline and protocol for appraising potential.**

> Economic value ≠ Potential

Scaleval is an open protocol for measuring, tracing and calibrating appreciations bearing on the potential of entities — people, products, companies, projects, territories — independently of their current economic value.

🌐 Website: [scaleval.org](https://scaleval.org)
📄 Official publications (DOI): see [Publications](#publications) below

---

## The Principle

An entity may have a low current economic value while presenting significant potential, and vice versa. Scaleval provides a vocabulary, units (**Potences**) and a protocol to make this potential appreciable, comparable and trackable over time — without ever confusing it with a currency or financial instrument.

The protocol operates through a five-step cycle:

> **Observed data → Appreciation → Potence → Future outcome → Calibration**

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Potence** | The unit of appreciation — an aggregated representation of convictions, never a currency |
| **Appreciary** | The real local ecosystem of a Potence (a country, region, or city) |
| **Potcap** | The appreciation capacity an appreciator holds and can commit in bills |
| **Reliability** | The quality of an appreciator's judgment, calibrated over time |
| **Scaleval Time** | Artificial calendar per Appreciary reflecting ecosystem vitality |
| **Potenance** | The domain organising the circulation and management of Potences |
